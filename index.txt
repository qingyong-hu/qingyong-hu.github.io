8:T713,I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Computer Science and Engineering at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, working with [Prof. Qian Zhang](https://home.cse.ust.hk/~qianzh/). I received my Ph.D. from HKUST in August 2025 and my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from the School of the Gifted Young at the **University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)** in July 2020, advised by [Prof. Wei Gong](https://ubiot.ustc.edu.cn/weigong/main.htm).

I build **low-burden sensing systems for everyday health monitoring**, combining wireless sensing and multimodal learning to assess movement, physiological signals, and cognitive health. My goal is to make healthcare more accessible through deployable, data-centric AIoT systems. Working with clinicians, I develop practical sensing systems, dedicated learning algorithms, and datasets that support evaluation in real-world settings.

My research agenda currently focuses on three thrusts:

- **AIoT for Human-centric Sensing**
- **AI for IoT System Optimization**
- **Robust and Scalable Machine Learning**
